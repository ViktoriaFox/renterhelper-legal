Who we are

This policy covers the RenterHelper iPhone app, made by Viktoria (“we”). Contact: viktoria.neva@protonmail.com.

RenterHelper keeps rental listings, questions, answers, notes and photos. This policy describes the current App Store version: data on the device, no account, no cloud we operate. Later versions may add paid features and optional sync — we will update this page before such a version ships, not after the fact.

What stays on the phone

You enter everything the app stores:

  • listings: title, address, price, currency, home type, phones, links, status;
  • checklist answers (Yes/No), per-question notes, the notes journal;
  • photos you add (JPEGs in the app sandbox, not in the system library);
  • settings: language, appearance, budget, whether to show the built-in checklist.

In this version we do not run a server for your listings and we do not receive a copy of them.

What goes on the network

  • Exchange rates. To show rent in hryvnia, the app fetches the official NBU rate from bank.gov.ua. The request does not include your listings, photos or notes. Like any website, NBU can see the device IP. The response is cached on the phone (up to 12 hours).
  • Maps. Tapping an address opens Apple Maps with that search string.
  • Links and phone numbers. Taps open Safari or Phone, only when you choose to.
  • Support email. “Contact support” opens your mail app to the address above.

This version does not send audio, transcripts or API keys to OpenAI and does not use the microphone.

What this version does not do

  • no account or login;
  • no ads, analytics or trackers; we do not sell data;
  • no sync of listings to a server we operate.

If iCloud Backup is on, a copy of the app’s data may be included in your iCloud Backup — that is Apple’s setting, not our server.

What may come later

Later versions may include paid features (through the App Store) and optional cloud sync so the same listings exist on more than one device. That is not in the build you download today. When it is, this page will say which data goes to a server, where that server is, and how to delete the copy. The core listing notebook may stay free.

Photos

Photo library access is used only when you pick pictures for a listing. Files stay in the app until you delete them.

Deleting data

  • delete a listing in the app;
  • Settings → “Clear all data”;
  • or delete the app — its sandbox goes with it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For this version of the app, the text here is the current one.
